Keyboard Shortcut

Trigger the optimizer without touching your mouse.

Ctrl+Shift+O
Mac: ⌘+Shift+O
To change this shortcut, visit — find "Optimize Prompt" and click the pencil icon.
  1. Click into any prompt field on Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini
  2. Type your rough idea or question
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+O (or click the ✨ button)
  4. Review the optimized prompt in the popup, edit if needed
  5. Click Use This to apply it
